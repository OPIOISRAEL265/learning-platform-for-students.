Key Problems and Solutions

Problem: Lack of Accessible Learning Platforms for Specific Subjects

Solution: A dedicated platform offering interactive simulations, curated content, step-by-step problem solvers, a community forum, and personalized learning paths.

Problem: Limited Access to Educational Resources in Geographic Areas

Solution: A virtual learning resource center with a digital library, online workshops, interactive learning games, offline access, and a community forum.

Problem: Inefficient Communication Between Teachers, Students, and Parents

Solution: A centralized communication portal with announcements, notifications, calendar integration, gradebook access, direct messaging, and discussion forums.

Problem: Difficulty in Tracking Student Progress and Identifying Learning Gaps

Solution: A learning analytics dashboard with automated assessments, progress tracking, learning gap identification, personalized recommendations, and reporting tools.

Problem: Difficulty in Connecting Students with Tutors

Solution: An online tutoring marketplace with tutor profiles, advanced search functionality, scheduling, video conferencing, and secure payment processing.
